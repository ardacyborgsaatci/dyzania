Privacy

Privacy note

Wrenbook is a fictional company made for a portfolio project. This note describes what the demo does with your details.

What the sales form collects

Your name, email address, company, company size, budget, timeline and an optional message, plus the time you sent it. They are used to reply and to score the enquiry. The form sends them to a Vercel function, which passes them to an n8n workflow that saves a row in a Google Sheet and, for high scores, emails the owner.

What the support demo collects

Text you type into the playground is processed by a Groq-hosted model through n8n, answered, and not stored. Only a hashed, salted form of your IP address is kept for an hour to enforce the rate limit. Please do not enter real personal data.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ad trackers. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page opens.

Deleting your details

Send a message through the sales form asking for deletion and give the email address you used.

Wrenbook is a fictional company made for a portfolio project. All people, companies and emails shown are invented. Nothing is ever sent to a customer automatically.
