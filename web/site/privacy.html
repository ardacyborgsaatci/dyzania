What this site does with what you type.

Hollis & Marr LLP is a fictional firm made for a portfolio project. Please use made-up details everywhere on this site. This page explains what happens to them anyway.

What the forms collect

The contact form

Your name, email, optional phone, how you want to be reached, the kind of matter, your description, where you are, whether a court date is coming, an optional date, an optional other side, and how you heard of the firm. The workflow checks it with fixed rules and saves it as a lead with a reference number. It replies with a fixed message.

The booking form

Your name, email, optional phone, the kind of matter, the other side (or that there is none), the time you chose, and the contact-form reference if you came from there. The booking is saved in the firm's booking table, and one confirmation email goes to the address you gave.

The voice line

Your speech is sent to Groq to be turned into text, the answer is written by a Groq chat model, and Fish Audio turns it back into speech. Details you say while booking stay out of the chat model's history.

Everything else

  • Logs. The workflows log references and categories, not names, email addresses or message text.
  • Cookies. This site sets none and does not use browser storage.
  • Fonts. The Inter typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when a page loads.
  • Hosting. The site runs on Vercel, which keeps its own request logs.
  • Abuse protection. To slow down scripts, the server keeps a short in-memory count of requests per IP address and checks a signed token that holds no personal data. A hidden field catches simple bots. The count is not stored.
  • How long data is kept. The activity log and the inbox log are set to be deleted after 90 days by a nightly job. Leads, bookings and court notices have no automatic deletion yet, and removal is done by hand. This is a demo.
  • Asking for removal. Write to privacy@hollismarr.example. The domain is a placeholder and the firm is fictional, so nobody reads that address.
