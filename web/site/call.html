Front desk

The voice line

A hands-free assistant you talk to like a phone call. It answers questions about plans, support hours and everyday tasks, using only the Wrenbook knowledge base.

What it can and cannot do

  • It answers from the knowledge base and says so when it does not know.
  • If you ask for a demo, it points you to the sales form. It never takes an email address by voice.
  • It cannot take payment details, change an account, reset two-factor, or approve a refund.

The voice agent runs as its own site because it needs a Python server. Its public page is not switched on yet, so there is nothing to call from here.

Talk to it

Allow the microphone when asked, speak, and wait for the reply. It answers in one or two sentences. Test version: speech is processed by Groq and Fish Audio.

Open it in its own tab if the microphone does not start here.

Wrenbook is a fictional company made for a portfolio project. All people, companies and emails shown are invented. Nothing is ever sent to a customer automatically.
