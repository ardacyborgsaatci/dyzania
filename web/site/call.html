The voice line

An automated assistant you talk to like a phone call. It answers questions about the firm and can book a first consultation by voice, through the same booking service as the website. It is not a lawyer.

Talk to it

The voice assistant runs as its own small server because it needs Python. It is not switched on for this site yet, so there is nothing to call from here. You can book on the website instead.

You are talking to an automated assistant, not a lawyer. Allow the microphone when asked, speak, and wait for the reply. Speech is processed by Groq (speech to text and the chat model) and Fish Audio (the voice), so use made-up details only.

Open it in its own tab if the microphone does not start here.

Court date, hearing, papers served on you, or a safety concern? Call the office on 555-0142 now instead of using a form. This site cannot tell you what your deadline is; only an attorney who has read your papers can.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number.

What it can do

  • Answer questions about practice areas, fees, hours, location and what to bring.
  • Offer three times for a first consultation, read back your details and book the one you choose.
  • Point you to a person at the office when you ask for one.

What it will not do

  • Give legal advice, say whether you have a case, or predict an outcome.
  • State a deadline. Anything time-sensitive gets a fixed reply that sends you to the office.
  • Change or cancel a booking. Call the office for that.
