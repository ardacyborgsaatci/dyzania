# About Wrenbook (fictional demo company)

Everything in this folder is invented for a portfolio project. Wrenbook is a small-business invoicing app. Facts here are read by the voice line and by the support reply drafts, so they must stay consistent with `02_help_center.md`.

## About Wrenbook
Wrenbook is a small-business invoicing and bills app, founded in 2019. Customers use it to send invoices, get paid online, and capture supplier bills. The team is 12 people: 2 in sales, 3 in support, 1 in finance, 5 engineers, and the founder, Priya Okafor. Wrenbook is a remote company with no public office.

## Contact and hours
- Support hours: Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 UTC.
- Support email: support@wrenbook.example.
- Sales email: sales@wrenbook.example.
- Billing email: billing@wrenbook.example.
- Security email: security@wrenbook.example. Only the security team handles these.
- Privacy email: privacy@wrenbook.example.
- Phone line: 555-0188, answered by the Wrenbook voice assistant at any hour. The assistant can answer questions but cannot take payment details, change an account, or approve a refund.
- Status page: https://status.wrenbook.example
- Outside support hours, leave an email and the team replies within the response times for your plan.

## Booking a demo
- A 30-minute demo can be booked at https://wrenbook.example/demo, or by emailing sales@wrenbook.example.
- Longer 45-minute sessions are available for teams that want to see integrations.
- Demos run Monday to Friday between 10:00 and 16:00 UTC, with at least 24 hours of notice.
- A person from sales confirms every booking by email.

## Talking to the voice assistant
- The assistant answers questions about plans, features, support hours and how to do common tasks.
- If a caller wants a demo, the assistant points them to the demo page or the sales email. It does not take email addresses by voice.
- If it does not know something, it says so and suggests emailing support.
